## Rhythm
- **0:00 cold open:** the product (our Tessera app-film shots) full screen, then into the split layout.
- **Talking parts:** the split layout above, with the panel showing what you're talking about.
- **Inserts:** when there's no screen action, the panel shows a clean product animation (white/cream), and you stay in the strip.
- **Cuts:** tight. Pauses, ums and loading screens removed.
- **Sound:** your voice first; music low underneath; small UI sounds only.

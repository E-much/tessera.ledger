# Tessera walkthrough — script & recording guide (~5 min)

Format: like the reference video. Screen recording in a big panel on the left, you on camera
in a tall strip on the right, word-by-word captions underneath, and quick cuts to full-screen
product shots. You talk in your own voice; I do the editing, captions, zooms, music and fixes.

---

## 1. Recording yourself (phone is fine)

**Camera**
- Phone's **back camera** if someone can help, front camera if alone. **Landscape**, 1080p or 4K, **30 fps**.
- Phone at **eye level** (stack books), about an arm and a half away. Frame **chest up**, face in the **middle** of the frame
  (I crop you into the tall strip, and sometimes use you full screen).
- Look at the lens, not the screen.

**Light** (this matters more than the camera)
- Sit **facing a window** in daytime, or put a lamp **behind the phone**, pointing at you.
- Never have a window or bright lamp **behind you**.
- A warm lamp somewhere in the background looks great (like the reference).

**Background**
- Anything works. I can **blur it** or **replace it** with a clean, soft backdrop. Just avoid people or a TV moving behind you.

**Sound** (the biggest quality jump)
- Best: wired earphones with a mic, or a cheap clip-on mic. Next best: a second phone recording voice memos, held close to your chest.
- Quiet room, fan and AC off, curtains or a bed nearby (soft things kill echo).
- **Clap once** at the start of each take (it helps me sync camera, mic and screen).

**Delivery**
- Read a chunk, look up, say it. You don't need it word-for-word. Your own words are better.
- Mess up? Pause, then redo the whole sentence. I'll cut the mistakes and the pauses.

## 2. Recording the screen

- Desktop browser, **full screen**, 1080p or higher. Hide bookmarks and extensions, close other tabs.
  Phone screen recordings also work (I'll put them in a phone frame).
- Move the mouse **slowly**, and pause 2 seconds on anything important.
- **One clip per chapter** (names like `03-signup.mov`). Don't worry about loading screens, I cut them.
- Don't show your email, wallet address, card details or balance unless you're OK with them on screen (I can blur things, just tell me).
- Upload the files here in the chat. If a file is too big, export it at 1080p.

## 3. Script

Timings are targets. **[SCREEN]** = what to record for that part. Lines marked **⚠︎ check** are things
only someone with access can confirm; adjust the line if the app works differently.

### 0:00 Hook (on camera, ~20s)
> What if you could own a piece of the companies everyone's talking about? SpaceX, OpenAI, Kalshi,
> the ones that aren't on the stock market yet. Normally that's impossible unless you're rich or
> connected. I just went through **Tessera**, a new app that changes that, and I'll show you exactly how it works, step by step.

[SCREEN] none. I open with a few seconds of the Tessera app film.

### 0:20 The problem (~35s)
> Here's the thing. The best companies are staying private longer than ever. And while they're private,
> regular people can't get in. You usually need to be an **accredited investor**, minimums can be
> **two hundred and fifty thousand dollars**, and your money can be locked up for years.
> So all the growth happens before the public ever gets a chance.

[SCREEN] none. I'll animate the problem (minimums, accreditation, lock-ups).

### 0:55 What Tessera is (~40s)
> Tessera gives you **exposure** to these private companies through tokens on Solana. They call them
> **T-tokens**: T-SpaceX, T-OpenAI, T-Kalshi.
> One important thing, and I like that they're upfront about it: a T-token is **not a share**.
> It's a *loan participation right*, issued by a subsidiary of the Tessera Works Foundation.
> So you're getting exposure linked to the company, not owning stock in it.

[SCREEN] `01-home` — tessera.pe homepage, scroll slowly top to bottom.

### 1:35 Signing up (~25s)
> Getting started is simple. You sign up with **Apple, Google or email**. That's it.
> And there's no accreditation check. You just need to be 18 or over, and outside the countries they exclude.

[SCREEN] `02-signup` — the sign-up / sign-in flow.

### 2:00 Browsing companies (~35s)
> Once you're in, you see the companies. Right now there are three: **SpaceX, OpenAI and Kalshi**.
> Tap one and you get the details: what the company does, where it's based, and the token info.
> And you can see more are on the way. There's a **"Coming soon"** spot, and you can hit *Notify me* to get an alert.

[SCREEN] `03-companies` — the companies list, open SpaceX, scroll its page, go back, show "Coming soon".

### 2:35 Adding funds (~40s)
> To buy, you first add funds. Tessera uses **USDC**, a digital dollar, and you can buy it right in the
> app **with your card or Apple Pay** through their payment partner, **Banxa**.
> Pick an amount, continue to Banxa, pay, and the USDC lands in your wallet.
> Heads up: the payment provider charges its own fees and runs its own verification.

[SCREEN] `04-add-funds` — Add funds, pick an amount, show the Banxa step. Stop before entering card details.

### 3:15 Buying your first token (~40s)
> Now the fun part. Pick a company, say SpaceX, and choose how much you want.
> You can start from **just one dollar**. Type it in, review the order, confirm.
> That's it. You now have T-SpaceX.

[SCREEN] `05-buy` — buy a small amount (a $1 test buy is perfect), the review screen, the confirmation.

### 3:55 Your wallet (~30s)
> Your tokens stay **in your own wallet**. Tessera doesn't hold them for you.
> T-tokens are **transferable on-chain**, but liquidity depends on the market, so it's not guaranteed you can sell when you want.
> **⚠︎ check:** if the app shows selling or a price chart, show it here and say how it works.

[SCREEN] `06-wallet` — the portfolio / wallet view after buying.

### 4:25 The fine print (~25s)
> Quick fine print, because this matters. These are not shares, and you get no voting rights.
> You can only **redeem** after what they call a *Liquidity Event*, like an IPO or acquisition, under their terms.
> It's **not available in the US, the UK, China** and a few other places. And like anything in investing,
> you could lose what you put in. So only use money you're OK with risking.

[SCREEN] `07-terms` — the Terms or FAQ page, scrolled briefly.

### 4:50 Wrap (on camera, ~15s)
> So that's Tessera: private-market exposure, from one dollar, in a few taps. And a new company is coming soon.
> Check it out at **tessera.pe**. Let me know in the comments which company you'd want next.

[SCREEN] none. I close on the Tessera logo and the full disclaimer.

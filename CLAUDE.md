# CLAUDE.md

## Motion design standard

The user's standing motion-design reference is **John C. Odunayo** (x.com/johncodunayo,
johnodunayo.my.canva.site). The full study lives in
[`docs/motion-reference/README.md`](docs/motion-reference/README.md), with frame sheets in
`docs/motion-reference/frames/`.

Whenever the user brings a new project, idea, product or campaign — even if they don't
mention motion — read that file and the relevant frame sheets, then propose what motion work
would fit it in his style, using the playbook in section 4 (mode, format, palette, beat
sheet, signature moments, assets needed, build notes). Adapt to the project's own brand;
don't copy his clients' footage or logos.
